The Platform

Adaptive Immunity,
Permanently Engineered

01
Engineer.
CRISPR Knock-In

We use precision CRISPR editing to insert therapeutic antibody genes directly into the immunoglobulin locus of human B cells — creating cells that are genetically reprogrammed to produce your target antibody.

02
Administer.
Once. Only Once.

Engineered B cells are differentiated into plasma cells and infused in a single treatment. No repeat injections. No maintenance dosing. The cells engraft and begin producing antibody immediately.

03
Protect.
For Life.

Long-lived plasma cells persist in bone marrow niches for decades, continuously secreting therapeutic antibody at clinically relevant concentrations. One treatment. Lifelong defense.

"The era of repeated injections is ending.
We are engineering the body to heal itself — permanently."

Adaptibody Therapeutics is pioneering the next paradigm of antibody medicine. Our B cell drug factory platform transforms a single cell therapy session into lifelong protection — targeting autoimmune disease, rare disorders, and chronic conditions where patients deserve a permanent cure.
